District Award

Pack 123 Wins 2026 District Award of Merit

Pack 123 from Chula Vista has been selected as this year's recipient of the Sweetwater District Award of Merit, recognizing outstanding unit service, membership growth of 22%, and exceptional youth programming throughout the 2025–26 scouting year.

Important Deadline

2025–26 Recharter deadline is June 30th

All units must complete recharter through my.Scouting.org no later than June 30, 2026. Units not rechartered by this date will be unable to participate in council activities until renewal is complete. Contact your Unit Commissioner with questions.

SDIC is hosting a Wood Badge course this summer. Registration is open to all registered adult leaders. Deadline to register is June 15th. Partial and full scholarships available — contact your District Executive.

Training & Resources

Everything Sweetwater District leaders need — curated and kept current

Required training

🛡️
Youth Protection Training Required
Mandatory for all registered adults. Must be renewed every 2 years. Complete online at MyScouting.org — takes approximately 90 minutes.
Take YPT →
🎓
Position-Specific Training Required
Den leaders, Cubmasters, and Scoutmasters must complete position-specific online modules before working with youth unsupervised.
View courses →
🏕️
Hazardous Weather Required
Required for any leader taking youth on an overnight camping activity. Short online course through MyScouting. Good for 2 years.
Take course →

Recommended training

🪵
Wood Badge
The premier leadership training experience for all registered adult leaders. Next SDIC course: July 18–20 & Aug 1–3, 2026.
Register →
🦅
NYLT — Youth Leadership
National Youth Leadership Training for Scouts 13+. Develops leadership skills in a scout-led patrol setting over 6 days.
Learn more →
⚙️
Baloo Training
Required for any adult who plans or conducts a Cub Scout pack overnight camping experience. One-day in-person course.
