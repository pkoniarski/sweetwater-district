Join Pack 123!

We welcome all youth in kindergarten through 5th grade. Come to a meeting and see for yourself — no commitment required.

📅

When We Meet

Den meetings are held every Monday evening at 6:30 PM during the school year. Pack meetings are the 4th Monday each month.

📍

Where We Meet

Heritage Elementary School, 1220 Avenida Cesar Chavez, Chula Vista, CA 91913. We meet in Room 14 — follow the Scout signs!

🎒

Who Can Join

Any youth in kindergarten through 5th grade (ages 5–10) living in or near Chula Vista. Girls and boys are welcome!

💰

What It Costs

Annual registration is $85 which includes national dues, council fees, and the Scout handbook. Uniform is additional. Financial assistance is available — just ask.

🔗

Official Registration

You can also register online at BeAScout.org — search for Pack 123 in Chula Vista to find us and begin the official application.

Where We Meet

Heritage Elementary School, Chula Vista

🗺️
Google Map Embed
Heritage Elementary School
1220 Avenida Cesar Chavez
Chula Vista, CA 91913

📅 Meeting Schedule

Den Meetings: Every Monday, 6:30–7:30 PM
Pack Meetings: 4th Monday each month, 6:30–8:00 PM
School Breaks: No meetings during CVESD holiday breaks

📍 Getting There

Enter from Avenida Cesar Chavez. Park in the main lot and look for Scout signs directing you to Room 14 at the back of the main building. Accessible parking is available near the main entrance.

🚗 Parking Notes

Parking is free in the school lot. The main lot fills quickly on meeting nights — the overflow lot on Heritage Road has additional spaces. Do not block the bus lanes on the east side.

♿ Accessibility

Room 14 is fully accessible via the main ramp entrance. Please contact us in advance if you need additional accommodations.
